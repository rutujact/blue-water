The Blue Water Toolkit
================

**A Framework for Modeling Net Watershed Impacts**

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.20665272.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20665272)

This repository contains the computational workflow and analytical
engine for the manuscript: \> **Hemingway, C. et al. (2026).**
*Accounting the unaccounted: a new framework for incorporating farm
social diversity in micro-regional water budgets* \[Journal Name\].
\[DOI\]

The **Blue Water Toolkit** provides a generalized R framework for
simulating Blue Water Potential (Recharge minus Irrigation requirement)
at the micro-regional or village scale. By coupling biophysical water
fluxes (driven by meteorological forcing and dynamic crop coefficients)
with agricultural demographics, this toolkit reveals how social
diversity of farms drive local groundwater sustainability.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To reproduce the findings of the manuscript or adapt this framework for
a new watershed, execute the scripts in the following order.

### Step 1: Data Preparation (`01_Format_Input_Data.Rmd`)

This script acts as a cookbook. It ingests raw site data (climate, soil,
crop phenology, and farm demographics) and translates it into the
standardized CSV formats required by the engine.

- **Adapting for your own data:** You do not need to format your raw
  data to match ours. Open this script and refer to the **Target Output
  Dictionaries** defined at the beginning of each section to see exactly
  how your final CSVs must be structured.

### Step 2: The Analytical Engine (`02_blue-water.Rmd`)

This is the primary computational workflow. It calculates effective
precipitation, simulates daily water budgets across all crop rotations,
aggregates the data by socio-economic farm typology, and generates the
final visualizations.

## 📊 Data Requirements

If applying this toolkit to a new region, you will need the following
localized datasets (see `01_Format_Input_Data.Rmd` for strict formatting
rules):

1.  **Daily Climate Forcing:** Precipitation and Reference
    Evapotranspiration ($ET_0$).
2.  **Soil Parameters:** Water Holding Capacity (WHC) and Readily
    Available Water (RAW) thresholds.
3.  **Crop Phenology:** Rooting depths and discrete or polynomial Crop
    Coefficient ($K_c$) curves.
4.  **Landscape Demographics:** Area (hectares) cultivated per crop
    system and the number of households belonging to each production
    system.
